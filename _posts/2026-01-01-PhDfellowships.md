We are excited to share that two PhD students from the DDPSE Lab have received prestigious graduate fellowship awards recognizing their research accomplishments.

- **Jacob Sweet** has received the **Eastman PhD Fellowship Award**, sponsored by Eastman. This fellowship recognizes outstanding graduate students whose research demonstrates strong potential for scientific impact and innovation.

- **Sidharth Laxminarayan** has received a ChBE Fellowship in recognition of his excellent research accomplishments and contributions to the School of Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering.

Congratulations Jacob and Sid!!!
