The Boukouvala Lab received a fellowship grant from the Renewable Bioproducts Insitute to support an incoming 2026 PhD student. The project will be co-led by Dr. Boukouvala and Dr. Sievers and is titled:"Design and Scale-Up of Mechanochemical Reactors for Cellulose Biorefining". More on RBI fellowships can be found [here](https://news.research.gatech.edu/2026/04/16/rbi-announces-new-fellowships-expanding-interdisciplinary-reach)
